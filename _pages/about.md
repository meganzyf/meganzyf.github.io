I'm Megan Zhao (赵一帆), a third-year Ph.D. student in physics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/), working with Prof. [Bhuvnesh Jain](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/people/standing-faculty/bhuvnesh-jain). I'm interested in extracting cosmological information with different probes and statistical methods. I currently work on weak lensing higher-order statistics as well as cross-correlating with CMB lensing. I also dabble in field-level inference, and more recently, calibration of Roman images. I'm a member of the Dark Energy Survey (DES) and the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope.\
\
I graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/en) in 2024 with a B.S. in astrophysics and a minor in theatre and performance studies. As an undergrad, I worked on machine learning in gravitational lensing and Type Ia supernovae, as well as a side quest into observing lensed quasars.\
\
Outside of research, I'm passionate about STEM [outreach](https://meganzyf.github.io/outreach) and theatre (both as an audience and as a [creator](https://taps.uchicago.edu/news-events/events/ba-projects)).

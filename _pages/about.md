I'm Megan, a second-year Ph.D student in physics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/), working with Prof. [Bhuvnesh Jain](https://www.physics.upenn.edu/people/standing-faculty/bhuvnesh-jain). I have a broad interest in cosmology. Right now, I'm working on using higher-order statistics to extract information from weak lensing survey data. I'm also involved in using field-level inference to infer cosmology. I'm a member of the Dark Energy Survey and the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope.\
\
I graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://www.uchicago.edu/en) in 2024 with a B.S. in astrophysics and a minor in theatre and performance studies. As an undergrad, I was interested in using machine learning to explore cosmological questions. You can check out my undergrad projects under the [research tab](https://meganzyf.github.io/research).\
\
Outside of research, I'm passionate about STEM [outreach](https://meganzyf.github.io/outreach) and theatre (both as an audience and as a [creator](https://taps.uchicago.edu/news-events/events/ba-projects)).
